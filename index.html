stream-clipper

A personal tool for turning Twitch VODs into short vertical clips.

What it does

stream-clipper reads the chat log and audio of a Twitch stream, finds the moments worth watching, and cuts them into vertical clips with subtitles. It runs on one person's own computer and uploads the finished clips to that person's own YouTube channel.

Who it is for

It is a single-operator tool, not a service. There are no accounts to create, no users other than its owner, and nothing to sign up for.

Google account access

The tool asks for one permission — youtube.upload — which allows it to upload videos to the owner's own channel and nothing else. It cannot read, edit or delete anything on the account.
